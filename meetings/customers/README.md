# Customers

Customer, prospect, and user calls — discovery, sales, success, support escalations. The YC "talk to users" record: this is typically the **highest-value category in the repo**, and the one agents read most when briefing.

- **Distill focus:** signals (what they said unprompted), asks and objections, commitments made by either side, verbatim quotes that carry weight, next step with owner.
- **Propagate — always:** every customer call appends a dated timeline entry to `customers/<slug>.md` (the entity, two levels up at the repo root — not this folder); compiled truth updated if a durable fact changed (plan, health, champion). Deal movement → `sales/pipeline.md`.
- **Don't:** paste call transcripts; include personal contact details beyond work email; editorialize signals into what you hoped they said.
