# Marketing

Marketing and growth syncs, campaign reviews, experiment readouts, positioning discussions.

- **Distill focus:** experiment results (hypothesis → outcome → keep/kill), campaign decisions, positioning/messaging changes, channel learnings.
- **Propagate:** messaging or positioning changes → `brand.md`; a growth bet adopted or killed → `strategy.md` active bets + `decisions/`; channel metrics stay in their dashboards — link, don't paste.
- **Don't:** record vanity-metric celebrations; a readout without a decision or learning is a dashboard, not a meeting note.
